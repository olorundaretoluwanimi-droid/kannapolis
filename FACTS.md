## Inferences and choices (marked on screen where shown)

- The 1920 French film "Filature et Tissage du coton" shows cotton machinery in general, not Kannapolis — marked "illustrative".
- "Ninety-seven years since the first mill houses" = 1906 → 2003.
- "Nearly eighty years" without a city government = 1906 → 1984.
- The Flickr postcards are undated (c. 1930s–40s).
